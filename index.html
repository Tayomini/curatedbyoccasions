Creative Direction

Event
Architect

Your vision, fully realized.

I

Before a single detail is decided,
there is a vision — and that starts here.

As your Event Architect, I work with you to build your event concept from the ground up — translating your ideas, personality, and aesthetic into a cohesive creative direction that feels entirely, unmistakably you.

From defining the mood and colour palette to curating outfit direction, styling references, and a realistic budget framework, I lay the complete creative foundation for your occasion. Every decision we make together ensures that when the day arrives, every element tells the same beautiful story.

What's included

  • Creative concept development
  • Mood board & aesthetic direction
  • Colour palette curation
  • Outfit & styling guidance
  • Budget framework & planning
  • Vision consultation
🌸

Design

Event Design
& Décor

Transforming spaces into experiences.

II

The atmosphere of your event should feel like it was designed for this exact moment.

Because it was. As your Event Designer, I develop the full visual concept for your occasion — the colour palette, floral direction, furniture, lighting, backdrops, tablescapes, and every detail that makes a space feel extraordinary.

Whether you have a clear vision or simply a feeling you want to evoke, I translate it into a cohesive, beautiful aesthetic that tells your story without you having to say a word. I handle sourcing, styling, and setup so the space is ready to take your breath away.

What's included

  • Mood board & concept design
  • Colour palette curation
  • Floral direction
  • Backdrop & prop styling
  • Sourcing & setup
  • Full teardown

Support

Event Concierge
Services

The behind-the-scenes hands.

III

The success of any great event lives in the details — and the details require running around.

That's where I come in. As your Event Concierge, I handle all the errands, pickups, drop-offs, and last-minute tasks leading up to and on the day of your event, so you never have to break a sweat.

From collecting items and picking up custom orders to setting up goodie bags and making sure everything is exactly where it needs to be — consider it handled. You stay present and enjoy the moment. I keep everything moving behind the scenes.

What's included

  • Pre-event errands & pickups
  • Day-of logistics support
  • Supply & prop collection
  • Last-minute tasks
  • Setup assistance
👑

On-Site

Event Day
Facilitator

Your calm in the celebration.

IV

Every great event needs someone who knows the plan, reads the room, and keeps the energy exactly where it needs to be.

As your Event Day Facilitator, I am your on-site presence from start to finish — hosting the event, welcoming and interacting with guests, guiding the flow of the evening, and making real-time decisions so nothing falls through the cracks.

This is more than logistics. I bring warmth, professionalism, and genuine care to every interaction — keeping the energy alive, the timeline on track, and the atmosphere exactly as you imagined. You get to be a guest at your own event. I make sure it runs beautifully.

What's included

  • Guest welcome & hosting
  • Event flow management
  • Real-time problem solving
  • Timeline execution
  • Full presence start to finish
📸

Creative

Photoshoot
Assistance

Helping you create images worth remembering.

V

A great photoshoot doesn't happen by accident — it's planned, styled, and directed with purpose.

As your Photoshoot Assistant, I work with you before the shoot to develop creative concepts, plan the shot list, and ensure every visual element is cohesive and intentional. On the day of, I am on site to coordinate styling, manage the flow of the shoot, and make sure the final images reflect exactly the story you want to tell.

Whether it's a birthday content shoot, a proposal re-creation, a brand session, or a styled event capture — I bring the creative direction and the on-the-ground support to make it all come together beautifully.

What's included

  • Pre-shoot concept development
  • Shot list planning
  • Styling direction
  • On-site coordination
  • Mood board creation
  • Photographer liaison
