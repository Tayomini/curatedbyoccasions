For Tayo, an event isn't finished until every last detail feels intentional.

With over four years spent bringing other people's celebrations to life, Tayo has built Curated by Occasions around a simple belief — the small things are the whole thing. As Lead Event Architect, she is the one translating a feeling into a fully realized vision, holding every colour, texture, and timing decision in her head until it clicks into place.

She is, by her own admission, obsessively detailed — the kind of planner who double-checks the placement of a place card and reworks a mood board until it feels right. But underneath the precision is the real reason she does this work: she loves creating wonderful memories, and she treats every event, big or small, as a moment someone will carry with them for years.
